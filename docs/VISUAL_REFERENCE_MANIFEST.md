## Reference-derived non-negotiables

1. Strand is a ribbon surface, not a glowing stroke.
2. Transverse segmentation is part of the material itself.
3. Twist is visible through large apparent-width changes and edge-on views.
4. Darker side/underside information preserves orientation.
5. Glow must not erase the geometry.
6. Multi-Strand scenes preserve identity through crossings.
7. Crossing alone is not a semantic junction.
8. Motion is tensioned and controlled rather than noisy, elastic, or organic.
